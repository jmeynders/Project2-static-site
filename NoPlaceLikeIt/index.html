No Place Like It

Living Anonymously

Introduction Statement

Every work in this exhibition is credited to an unknown artist. These are pictures of somewhere; Created by anonymous artists painting pictures living from somewhere else — not so much as dreams of travel but of an idealistic dream through secondhand imagining. These works are grouped into five "rooms," they show the anonymous dreams of the ideal place and why there is No Place Like It.

The museum organizes these works by chronology and medium. Each of these five thematic "rooms" promise a different version of an ideal life. Because the artists are anonymous, these landscapes belong to no one in particular. That anonymity of an author allows these places to stand in for someone's dream of the perfect home. And yet, looking at them side-by-side, we can ask: whose dream was this, and why does it look so much like Italy?
